Large dots frighten ghosts. Eating them sends jammers sideways.

Arrows or WASD to move. Eat a large dot, then a blue ghost, to throw jammers at the side boards. The fruit under the ghost house advances the board. Clearing every pellet speeds you up instead. White jammers slow you down and die if they touch you; a power pellet wipes them. Red jammers kill on contact, freeze while a power pellet is active, and vanish when you eat the fruit. Red fill is pressure. An X means that opponent is out. R restarts.
